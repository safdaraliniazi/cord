Build by coordinates

In Cord City you place every road and building by its map coordinates. The yellow numbers along the map edges are X, and the blue numbers are Y. Bright grid lines mark every 10 tiles.

  1. Pick a tool from the toolbar.
  2. Enter From X, Y. For roads, power lines, zones and bulldozing, also enter To X, Y to cover a line or a rectangle.
  3. Check the preview on the map, then press Build. After a road segment is built, its end point becomes the next segment's start.

You can also type commands in the console. Separate several commands with ;:

coal 30 36
road 20 30 to 40 30
res 21 26 to 30 29
com 21 31 to 26 33
ind 34 31 to 39 34
park 31 26
bulldoze 25 27 to 26 28
goto 32 32
pause / play / help

Other buildings: wind, police, fire, school, line (power line), trees, inspect X Y. Made a mistake? Press ↶ Undo or type undo to take back the last build and get your money back.

Roads connect your city. Zones only work together through connected roads: homes need a road route to jobs (commercial or industrial zones), and businesses need homes on the same road network. Zones marked ⇄ have no partners connected. Every worker drives to the nearest job, so busy roads jam. Jams slow growth and add pollution. Use the Traffic overlay to find them, then build another route around.

Growing the city: zones also need power, a road within 3 tiles, and demand (the R/C/I bars). Parks, schools, police and fire stations raise land value. Keep homes away from factory smoke.

Moving the map: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap a tile to see its coordinates and details. Keys: / focuses the console, ↑ and ↓ recall earlier commands, Space pauses, Esc clears.
