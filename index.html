How to build your city

  1. Build a power source: a coal plant or wind turbines.
  2. Lay roads. Drag to draw them. Roads also carry power.
  3. Zone land near roads: Residential, Commercial, Industrial. Drag to paint an area.
  4. Zones grow when they have power, a road within 3 tiles, and demand (see the R/C/I bars).
  5. Add parks, schools, police and fire stations to raise land value, which lets buildings grow taller.
  6. Keep housing away from factory pollution. Watch your budget and taxes.

Controls: left-drag builds · right/middle-drag or WASD pans · scroll or pinch zooms · Space pauses · Esc cancels · 1–9 select tools
