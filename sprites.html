Cord City Sprites

Starter pixel-art set: one design for every building, plus one per density level for homes, shops and industry so you can see them grow. Everything is drawn at 32×16 pixels per tile and scaled up with sharp pixels.

Zoom Water and turbines animate

Smoke, cars, fire and power-line wires stay animated in code on top of these sprites. Once you confirm, these replace the current box graphics in the game.
